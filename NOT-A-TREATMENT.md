# This is not treatment

Steady is a support tool. It is not therapy, not medical advice, not a crisis service,
and not a substitute for a clinician. It makes no claim to treat, cure, or prevent
addiction or any condition.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line
for your country — findahelpline.com lists verified lines by country.

Steady is deliberately built so that it cannot become surveillance: the partner-facing
signal carries no content, ever.
