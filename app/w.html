You will never see what she wrote. Not the words, not how bad it got, and not whether she slipped. That is not a limitation I ran out of time to build — it is the whole reason she can be honest with this thing.

This page only ever tells you whether she checked in recently. She chose to share it. She can stop at any time by not using the link.

Steady is a support tool, not treatment and not a monitoring service. It cannot tell you she is in danger, and it is not a substitute for a conversation. If you are frightened and need someone for you, findahelpline.com lists verified lines by country.
